Mockup notes (not part of the app)

  • Two steps: "Check Outlook" only looks (nothing goes to the AI). You see the counts and what was skipped, then click "Read" to send it to the selected AI. That way you can prove it out before spending time or tokens.
  • Since: defaults to your last import (7 days on the first run). Pick "Last 30 days" or a custom date to go further back. Mail that was already read is never read twice, so going back only adds the older mail.
  • Per conversation: the AI reads each conversation as a whole (received and sent messages together), so it knows whether you already replied and who still owes you an answer.
  • Skipped: newsletters and automated senders, meeting invites, and conversations where your reply is the latest message (unless something in them is still open). "Read anyway" sends a skipped one through.
  • Recent imports: each mail import is one row, so 40 conversations don't push your pasted imports off the list. Every conversation is still kept in the archive with its own AI log.

Mockup notes (not part of the app)

  • Grouped by conversation: each grey header is one email conversation. "Open in Outlook" opens that message in Outlook.
  • Needs reply is a normal task ("Reply to …") with its own pink chip, so it's ranked by the priority score with your other work. The requester is the sender.
  • Waiting on comes from your sent mail when you asked someone for something. After 3 business days with no reply it climbs in priority as a follow-up (the score already counts staleness).
  • Reply arrived: when someone answers a question you're waiting on, Review proposes clearing it, and may add a task if their answer gives you something to do.
  • Keys still work: J/K to move, A accept, R reject, Enter edit.
