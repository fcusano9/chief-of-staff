Mockup notes (not part of the app)

  • Just paste and Extract. Type pills, Title and From are gone. The date stays at the top right (today by default); set it for something older and the AI reads "Friday" as the Friday after that date (new: today relative dates always count from today).
  • The AI names it with Claude or Ollama (e.g. "Dealer pricing sync, Nov 6"); Basic uses the first line; a dropped file uses its file name.
  • Where the Copilot prompt lives (switch at the top): A its own "Copilot prompts" card on Import, between the mail card and the text card; B a "Copilot Prompt Generator" button at the top right of Import that opens a pop-up (Esc or Copy closes it). Either way it's separate from the text box.
  • Copilot prompt makes the Teams chats or Teams meetings prompt (from docs/COPILOT-PROMPTS.md) with exact dates instead of "the last 7 days". It starts where your last import of that prompt ended, so nothing is pulled twice and nothing is missed. Copy it, paste it into Copilot, then paste Copilot's answer here.
  • How the app knows what was covered: the prompt asks Copilot to start its answer with a line like "Chief of Staff · Teams chats · 2026-11-07 15:14 to 2026-11-08 09:30". When you Extract text with that line, the app records the range (only once it's imported, so a prompt you copied but never used doesn't skip anything), names the import "Teams chats, Nov 7 – 8" and dates it. The first time, it starts 7 days back. You can change the start.
  • Rename later: hover a row in Recent imports and click the pencil to change the title.
  • Primary button in dark mode, newest options: C a light grey (#c9c9d1) with dark text, and D a pale, low-saturation version of the accent purple (#bcbcdc) with dark text. Both are clearly lighter than the surroundings but not bright white. Light mode keeps today's black button.
  • Earlier options (all screens, not just Import): today it's bright white. Option A uses the accent purple in both themes; option B keeps black-on-light in light mode and a lighter grey button with white, slightly bolder text in dark mode, so it still stands out from the others. Toggle above, and try Light / Dark.
  • Other changes: the card says what to do ("Paste or drop text"), the whole text box accepts dropped files, the character count shows once there's text, and the long privacy sentence becomes "Claude Opus 5.5 · sent to Anthropic", matching the mail card. Ctrl+Enter still extracts.
