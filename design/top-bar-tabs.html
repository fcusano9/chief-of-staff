What changes
  • Today, Tasks, Waiting, Weekly | Projects, People on the left. Import and Review in a boxed group by the search box.
  • Review shows only while there is something to review. If you accept the last item while on Review, you stay on it (it says you're all caught up) and the tab goes when you leave. G then R still opens Review when it's empty.
  • ← and → step through the tabs in the order shown, skipping Review when it's hidden. They stop at the ends instead of wrapping round. The status bar shows "← → tabs".
  • They do nothing while you're typing (text boxes, dropdowns, sliders), while a window or the palette is open, or while the divider between list and details has focus (it uses ← → to resize). On Settings, which isn't a tab, they do nothing.
  • Prep and Archive are hidden until they're built. New shortcut: G then I for Import.
