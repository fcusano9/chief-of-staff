Mockup notes (not part of the app)

  • Order: new projects and people come first, then context for existing ones, then tasks, so the links read top to bottom.
  • Links: the dashed chips mean "not created yet". Accept a task with one and the project or person is created with it. Reject the project or person and the task just loses the link.
  • Context: "Project context" and "Person context" replace today's "Brief update" label. The text is added to the brief with the date and source, as it is today.
  • New people start at 3 stars. Edit to change their name, role, email or what's added to their context.
  • Keys still work: J/K to move, A accept, R reject, Enter edit.
