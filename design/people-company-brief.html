Mockup notes (not part of the app)

  • Company is a new field, above Role. It shows under each name next to the role and suggests companies you've already used as you type. When an import says what company a new person is from, the New person suggestion in Review fills it in. It's never guessed.
  • Filters: pick a company, or "No company". Open asks shows people with open tasks they asked you for, or tasks you're waiting on them for. The number on each row counts both. Filters are remembered.
  • Brief is one sentence about who the person is and what they do (two at most), shown as plain text right under their name. Regenerate asks the AI for a new one, which shows as a suggestion you keep or discard. Edit lets you rewrite it yourself; it saves on its own, separate from the Save button below.
  • "New notes since this brief" appears when context has been added since the brief was written, so you know when it's worth regenerating. Try Alex Rivera.
  • Context history is what used to be the brief box: every dated note from Review, plus anything you type. Nothing is lost. Existing briefs move here, and people start with no brief until you click Write brief.
  • What the AI reads on each import: the person's name, role, company and one-line brief. Today it reads the first 200 characters of the history box, which are the oldest notes.
