Mockup notes (not part of the app)

  • Same as Windows up to the last step: the AI writes the first draft, you check To, Cc and the text, Try again with a note, or Discard.
  • Two ways to finish, on both computers (Windows says Outlook, the Mac says Mail): Open in … opens a reply window with the text above the quoted email, for you to finish, save or send; Save to … Drafts saves it to Drafts without opening anything (today's Outlook behavior). Neither ever sends. Keep both, or drop one after trying them.
  • Not yet proven on Apple Mail: Save to Mail Drafts. Mail's scripting lists saving but doesn't fully define it, so it gets tested first (one test draft in your Drafts, with your OK). If it doesn't work, the Mac keeps only Open in Mail.
  • Activity gets "Reply opened in Outlook/Mail" or "Draft reply saved to Outlook/Mail Drafts". The task stays open.
  • Newest email: as on Windows, the reply goes on the newest email in the conversation, sent or received, so follow-ups stay in the chain.
  • To and Cc are worked out from the email itself (Apple Mail can't preview them), so check them in the Mail window.
