Mockup notes (not part of the app)

  • Only when you click. Nothing runs on its own. Write briefing (or Refresh once there is one) asks the selected AI. It's also in the Ctrl+K palette as "Write briefing". There's no keyboard shortcut for it.
  • Full width (the default here) puts the briefing between the number cards and the Focus list, with its sections side by side. Side column is the earlier version, for comparison.
  • Three short sections: a one-line summary, then Top priorities, Waiting-on nudges, and New since your last briefing. Each item opens its task.
  • Overdue tasks are part of Top priorities and listed first, with a red "Overdue" tag and a red edge so they stand out. Tasks due today or soon say so in their reason.
  • Empty sections stay on the page with a short line saying there's nothing there, so the layout doesn't jump around. Try "Nothing to report".
  • Where items come from: The reasons come from the AI; the items themselves always come from your real task list, so a briefing can't mention a task that doesn't exist.
  • Text only. The briefing never changes anything and never sends anything to Review. If it recommends something, that's a sentence in the briefing.
  • "New since last briefing" covers tasks, waiting-on items and Review suggestions added since the previous one. Your first briefing looks back one day.
  • Every briefing is saved with its date. History opens past ones, newest first. They're kept in the local database and included in the daily backup. Today's briefing stays on the page until you refresh it; an older one gets a "From yesterday" note so it isn't mistaken for today's.
  • The AI log records each briefing request and response, like imports.
  • Ready for a schedule later. Each saved briefing records how it was started ("You clicked" now). Adding a schedule later means a time setting in Settings that starts the same briefing; the Today card, History and storage don't change. Nothing for a schedule is visible in this version.
